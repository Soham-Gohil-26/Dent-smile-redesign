About the clinic

A small practice,
by design.

01

Dr. Muskan Majgaonkar

BDS · Founder & Lead Dental Surgeon

Dr. Muskan founded Dent-O-Smile with one intention: to run the kind of dental clinic she would want her own family to visit. Calm, clean, and completely honest about what treatment is needed and what it will cost.

She holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) and has completed a Masterclass in Direct Composite Bonding, a discipline focused on restoring teeth naturally and conservatively.

Every consultation at the clinic is handled by Dr. Muskan personally, including the phone call that books it. Patients are not passed between assistants, and nothing is recommended that is not genuinely required.

02

How we work

We explain first

You will see your X-ray and hear exactly what is happening in plain language, before any decision is made.

The price is fixed

You are told the full cost upfront. It does not change once treatment begins.

Comfort is the priority

Gentle technique, proper anaesthesia and as few sittings as clinically sensible.

Only what you need

If a tooth can be saved simply, we save it simply. No unnecessary treatment, ever.
